# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing AdventureWorks sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand overall business performance, product trends, and customer purchasing behavior.

The interactive dashboard provides insights into revenue, profit, orders, return rates, product performance, and customer segmentation to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Insights

- Overall revenue and profit performance.
- Monthly revenue trends and sales growth.
- Sales and order distribution across product categories.
- Top-performing products based on revenue and orders.
- Product return rate and customer purchasing behavior.
- Customer segmentation based on income level and occupation.
- Identification of high-value customers.
- Monthly product performance and profitability trends.

## Business Objective

To analyze AdventureWorks sales performance and identify key business trends, profitable products, customer behavior, and opportunities to improve revenue and profitability.

## Business Questions Answered

1. What is the overall revenue and profit generated?
2. How are sales and revenue trending over time?
3. Which product categories generate the highest number of orders?
4. Which products contribute the most revenue?
5. Which products have the highest return rates?
6. Who are the top customers based on revenue?
7. How do customer income levels influence purchasing behavior?
8. How does product profitability change over time?
9. Which products require attention based on returns and performance?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides an overview of business performance through key performance indicators and interactive visualizations.

- Total Revenue: $25M
- Total Profit: $10M
- Total Orders: 25K
- Return Rate: 2.17%
- Revenue trends and category-wise order analysis.
- Top 10 products by orders, revenue, and return percentage.

### 2. Product Detail Dashboard

Analyzes individual product performance and profitability.

- Monthly orders, revenue, and profit against targets.
- Product-level performance analysis.
- Dynamic price adjustment parameter.
- Revenue, profit, returns, orders, and return percentage analysis.
- Monthly profitability trends.

### 3. Customer Detail Dashboard

Provides insights into customer purchasing patterns and segmentation.

- Unique Customers: 17K
- Revenue per Customer: $1.43K
- Customer segmentation by income level.
- Customer distribution by occupation.
- Top customers based on revenue.
- Customer-level orders and revenue analysis.
